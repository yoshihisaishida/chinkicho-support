Chinkicho Privacy Policy

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how BOTANICAL TIMES (the “Operator”) handles user information in Chinkicho (the “Service”). The official service region is Japan. Selecting a display language does not change the official service region.

The Service collects or generates the following information only as necessary.

Profiles, posts, comments, images, public IDs, and external URLs are visible to signed-in users. If required information is not provided, the user may be unable to create an account or use the relevant feature. Optional profile fields may be left blank.

Apple processes payment information. Neither the Operator nor RevenueCat receives credit card numbers or other payment-method details. Chinkicho sends its user ID to RevenueCat, but does not send email addresses, profile text, posts, or photos. This ID can be linked to a Chinkicho account and is not treated as anonymous data.

Ordinarily, only the two participants can view DM text. The Service does not provide end-to-end encryption (E2EE) that prevents the Operator from reading messages. When a report is made, the reported message and necessary preceding messages selected by the reporter are retained as evidence and reviewed by authorized operators for handling the report. DM text is not used for advertising, recommendation analysis, or AI training. The Service does not automatically monitor all DM text. Push notifications do not include message text or the other participant’s name. Disclosures required by law and similar circumstances are limited to Section 4.

3. Age Confirmation, Images, and Location

  1. A date of birth entered for age confirmation is evaluated only on the device and is not sent to or stored on the Operator’s servers. The Operator stores only a record confirming that the user is aged 18 or older.
  2. The Service does not provide a feature that uses precise location information.
  3. Uploaded images are corrected for orientation, stripped of EXIF, GPS, and other metadata, and converted for display. For a safety check before publication, a resized image with its metadata removed is sent to Google Cloud. The original pre-processing image is deleted when processing succeeds or the post is discarded. If processing fails or is interrupted, it is deleted within 24 hours after upload.
  4. The Service does not collect advertising IDs, persistent device identifiers, or browsing history for behavioral advertising.

  2. The primary processing location for app data, image storage and conversion, and the administration website is Japan. 5. Retention and Account Deletion

The Operator does not retain information longer than necessary for its purposes. Principal retention periods are as follows.

Information may be retained only to the extent permitted by applicable law when necessary for legal obligations, protection of rights, abuse prevention, or unresolved safety matters.

Purchase and subscription information is retained during the 30-day account deletion cancellation period and is not deleted if the request is canceled within that period. After the period ends, we request deletion from RevenueCat and verify completion. Failed processing is retried, and unresolved failures are reviewed by the Operator. Canceling, expiring, or refunding Premium alone does not delete the account’s purchase history.

Payment and subscription records held by Apple are handled under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. Deleting a Chinkicho account or RevenueCat purchase data does not stop Apple’s automatic renewal.

The 365-day DM period is the ordinary viewing limit; it does not mean all copies in report evidence or recovery backups are erased at the same instant. Report evidence follows the safety-related retention period above. Backup copies are not used for ordinary viewing, and recovery is handled so that expired or deleted text is not displayed again.

6. User Choices and Rights

  1. Users can manage public profile information, notifications, blocks, and account deletion in the app.
  2. Subject to applicable law, users may request notice of purposes, access, disclosure, a copy, correction or supplementation, cessation of collection, processing or use, or deletion of their personal information.
  3. Signed-in users may submit a request through “Contact Us” in the app. Persons who cannot sign in and non-users may use the public support form, which does not require sign-in. The Operator may verify the requester’s identity.
  4. A request may be denied in whole or in part where an exception under law applies or where necessary to protect another person’s rights or safety. The Operator will provide a reason as required by applicable law.
  5. The Operator does not sell or share user information for advertising. Any additional rights granted by applicable law remain unaffected.

DMs are optional. Users can turn off receiving or DM notifications, or block another user. Hiding a conversation from the inbox does not delete the other participant’s history.

7. Security Measures

The Operator takes security measures appropriate to the nature and risk of the information, including protection in transit and at rest, access restrictions, management of providers, log review, and removal of image metadata, to prevent leakage, loss, alteration, or misuse.

8. Persons Under 18

The Service is intended for persons aged 18 or older. If the Operator becomes aware that a person under 18 is using the Service, it may take necessary action, including suspension or deletion of the account.

9. Changes and Contact

  1. If there is a material change to the information collected, purposes of use, or user rights, the Operator will provide notice in accordance with applicable law and obtain consent where required.
  2. Signed-in users may contact the Operator about this Policy or user information through “Contact Us” in the app. Persons who cannot sign in and non-users may use the public support form, which does not require sign-in.
