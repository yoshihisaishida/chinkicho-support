Chinkicho Community Guidelines

Effective date: the date the Service is made generally available

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. About These Guidelines

Chinkicho aims to be a place where people who enjoy rare and unusual plants can safely share photographs, knowledge, and cultivation records. These Guidelines set the standards of conduct for Chinkicho, a social networking service provided by BOTANICAL TIMES (the “Operator”).

The Service is intended for persons aged 18 or older.

2. Respect Others

Respect other users and post only content that you have the right to share. Expressing an opinion about a plant’s condition, market price, or cultivation method is not itself a violation, but attacks, harassment, or infringement of another person’s rights are not allowed.

Do not include home addresses, telephone numbers, payment details, shipping addresses, or other personal information that should remain private in posts, comments, or profiles.

This does not categorically prohibit posting a publicly available shop or venue address or official telephone number of a plant shop or plant event for informational purposes. Do not post a person’s home address, private telephone number, or information that has not been made public.

3. Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following content and conduct are prohibited.

  1. Violating the law or facilitating unlawful conduct
  2. Infringing copyright, trademark, portrait rights, privacy, or other rights
  3. Harassment, bullying, discrimination, hate, threats, violence, or encouragement of such conduct
  4. Encouraging self-harm, dangerous conduct, sexual content, sexual exploitation, or harm to child safety
  5. Repeatedly posting identical or substantially identical sales posts in large numbers, or advertising sales on unrelated posts
  6. Impersonation, fraud, false sales claims, spam, phishing, malware, or other dangerous external redirection
  7. Soliciting pyramid schemes, gambling-like sales schemes, or other improper transactions
  8. Soliciting the sale, transfer, or exchange of stolen goods, unlawfully collected plants, or other prohibited items
  9. Intentionally misrepresenting a plant’s source, variety, origin, or condition, or using another person’s photograph without permission
  10. Evading suspension or other enforcement measures, or interfering with operation of the Service

4. Transactions Outside the Service

Statements of an intention to sell, a price, condition, shipping region, external shop URL, or public social media contact are not categorically prohibited. However, Chinkicho does not recommend such listings or transactions.

Chinkicho does not provide listings that accept purchase applications and form sales contracts, orders, payments, shipping, refunds, or dispute mediation. Transactions must take place outside the Service based on the users’ own judgment and responsibility. Do not post home addresses, telephone numbers, payment details, shipping addresses, or other private information on Chinkicho.

When sending plants across national borders, users must check applicable plant quarantine, import and export, customs, CITES, and other legal requirements themselves.

5. Reporting, Blocking, and Emergencies

Users can report a problematic post, comment, or user in the app. Blocking a user limits mutual viewing and interaction. Information identifying a reporter will not be disclosed to the reported user, except where disclosure is required by law, legal proceedings, or a lawful request from a public authority.

If there is an immediate real-world danger, contact the police, fire service, emergency medical services, or another local emergency service before reporting it to Chinkicho.

6. Enforcement and Appeals

When necessary to address a violation of the Terms of Use or these Guidelines, ensure safety, or comply with law, the Operator may hide or remove content, issue a warning, request changes to displayed content, or suspend an account. A serious violation may result in an indefinite suspension on the first occurrence.

A user subject to an enforcement measure designated by the Operator as eligible for appeal may appeal within the period and through the method specified by the Operator. Any separate procedure required by applicable law remains available. If the Operator finds that the measure was incorrect, it will restore the content or account status where possible. The Operator may withhold part of the reason for a measure where necessary to protect the safety or rights of a reporter or third party, an investigation, or legal requirements.

7. Reports Concerning Age or Infringement

A user believed to be under 18, or an infringement of copyright, trademark, portrait rights, privacy, or other rights, may be reported in the app. Persons who cannot sign in and non-users may use the public support form, which does not require sign-in.

8. Changes and Contact

The Operator may change these Guidelines when necessary. Changes that materially affect users’ rights or obligations or the content users may post will be notified in accordance with applicable law, and users will be asked to consent again where required.

Signed-in users may contact the Operator about these Guidelines through “Contact Us” in the app. Persons who cannot sign in and non-users may use the public support form, which does not require sign-in.
